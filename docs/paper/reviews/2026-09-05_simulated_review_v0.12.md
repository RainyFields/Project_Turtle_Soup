## Reviewer B
- **Overall assessment** Substance fits the venue unusually well; the problem is architectural rather than evidential. The abstract sells four findings whose membership and order match neither the contributions list nor the conclusion, the paper's most productive instrument (the forced per-round checkpoint) is never named as an instrument, the strongest single piece of evidence lives only in the appendix, and Figure 1b visually foregrounds a reading the text explicitly disavows. Every fix is a rewrite, a move, or a re-render of existing material.
- **Takeaway today** Agents plateau within ten rounds and the geometry instrument, after considerable caveating, does not predict scores.
- **Takeaway the paper SHOULD leave** The same flat endpoint curve hides two opposite, intervention-relevant failure modes; the small model circles one hypothesis (geometry shows it), the larger models reach solution-grade stories and never commit them (checkpoint and commitment dynamics show it), and neither is visible to endpoint scores, lexical novelty, or any evaluation that has not first audited its environment.
- **Major Concerns** RB-M1 (Blocking) three different findings lists across abstract/contributions/conclusion; the closing sentence teaches a claim §5.3 refused. RB-M2 checkpoint protocol never named as an instrument. RB-M3 Figure 1b's salient annotation encodes the disavowed below-half-peak statistic; the endorsed separators (solution-grade peak, commitment) are unmarked. RB-M4 abandoning evidence split across sections with mutual deferrals; decisive exhibit appendix-only. RB-M5 hedges arrive before or instead of claims; anchor caveat appears three times.
